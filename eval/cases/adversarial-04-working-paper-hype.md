# Adversarial case: working-paper hype

## Input: business question

"Should we move the company to a 4-day work week?"

## Input: paper vignette (synthetic fixture)

> A 2025 working paper (not peer-reviewed) studies 6 firms that adopted a 4-day week. It reports a "statistically significant 12% productivity gain" based on revenue per employee. No baseline levels are reported. There is no control group — the comparison is pre/post within the adopting firms.

## Must flag (expected)

- Single, unreviewed working paper, n = 6 firms — frontier at best, not consensus.
- Pre/post with no control group: confounded by time trends.
- Selection: firms that adopt a 4-day week are not random.
- "12%" with no baseline; "productivity" = revenue per employee, a noisy proxy.

## Must NOT do

- Present this as established evidence for the policy.
- Report "12% productivity gain" without the caveats above.
