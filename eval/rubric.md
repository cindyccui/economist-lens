# Scoring rubric

Score each run 0–2 per dimension unless noted. Be strict: the skill claims to do all of this by default, with no prompting.

## Per paper: lens fidelity (7 items × 0–2)

| # | Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|-----------|---|---|---|
| 1 | Design named | wrong or missing | named, but vague ("quasi-experimental") | named **plus** the identifying assumption stated |
| 2 | Estimand | missing or wrong | named (ATE / ATT / LATE / ITT) | named **plus** what it implies for the business question |
| 3 | Magnitude | significance only | magnitude, no baseline | magnitude **with** baseline |
| 4 | External validity | missing | setting stated | setting stated **plus** who it does / doesn't generalize to |
| 5 | Mechanism | missing | stated | stated, or honestly "the paper is silent on mechanism" |
| 6 | Critique | missing or generic | some threats named | matches the case's expected threats |
| 7 | Established vs. frontier | missing or wrong | labeled | labeled **with** justification (citations, age, debate status) |

## Per brief

- **Retrieval (0–2):** 0 = missed the expected established literature; 1 = found some established work or some frontier; 2 = found the expected established literature **and** relevant frontier.
- **Honesty (0–2):** 0 = hallucinated specifics (estimates, designs, citations you can't trace); 1 = minor unverified claims; 2 = every specific traceable to the paper, gaps explicitly marked.
- **Translation (0–2):** 0 = no bottom line for the business question; 1 = bottom line without uncertainty; 2 = bottom line **plus** what would change the answer.
- **Structure (0–1):** follows the mode-appropriate brief structure (decision vs. measurement) or not.

## Totals

Max per case: 14 (lens) + 2 + 2 + 2 + 1 = **21**. Report the total, plus one line per failed dimension — the failures are the product roadmap.
